## 2. Root Cause & Resolution of POST Hanging
- The original InputValidationMiddleware mutated `request.scope["query_string"]` and did not always reset the request body correctly after reading it.
- This caused FastAPI/Starlette to hang on POST requests with a body, as the body was consumed and not available for downstream parsing.
- Refactored InputValidationMiddleware to:
  - Never mutate `request.scope["query_string"]`.
  - Always set `request._body` after reading and sanitizing the body.
  - Always call `await call_next(request)` exactly once.
  - Add robust error handling and logging.
- After this fix, POST requests with all middleware enabled work as expected.
